Implement `ShippingQuotes` in `src/shipping_quote_service/__init__.py`.

`quote(weight_grams, destination_zone, *, service="standard", po_box=False)` accepts standard domestic parcels. Weight must be a positive integer and zone must be 1, 2, or 3. Standard rates in integer cents by `(zone, weight band)` are: zone 1 = `(500, 800, 1200)`, zone 2 = `(700, 1100, 1600)`, zone 3 = `(900, 1400, 2100)`, for weights up to 1,000g, up to 5,000g, and above 5,000g respectively. P.O. boxes are explicitly accepted.

Return `{"service": "standard", "charge": cents, "billable_weight_grams": weight}`. Reject invalid input or unsupported services with `ValueError`.
