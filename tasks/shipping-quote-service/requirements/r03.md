Revise billable-weight calculation to account for parcel dimensions.

`quote` accepts optional `dimensions_cm=(length, width, height)`, where every dimension is positive. Dimensional weight in grams is `ceil(length * width * height / 5)` (the 5,000 cm3/kg carrier divisor). Bill and return the greater of actual and dimensional weight. Omitted dimensions use actual weight. Packages whose dimensional weight is smaller retain their previous charge. Reject malformed dimensions with `ValueError`.
