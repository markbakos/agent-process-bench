Extend quoting with express service.

Callers select `service="standard"` or `service="express"`. Express rates for the same three bands are: zone 1 = `(900, 1400, 2100)`, zone 2 = `(1200, 1800, 2700)`, zone 3 = `(1500, 2300, 3400)`. Return the selected service. Standard behavior and P.O. box acceptance remain unchanged.
