Extend appointment booking and rescheduling with variable durations.

`book` accepts keyword `duration_minutes`, limited to 30, 60, or 90 and defaulting to 30. The entire duration must fit inside operating hours and be free of overlap. `reschedule` accepts an optional replacement `duration_minutes`; when omitted it preserves the appointment's current duration. Reject other durations with `ValueError` without modifying the schedule.
