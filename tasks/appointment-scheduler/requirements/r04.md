Extend `Scheduler` with weekly recurring booking.

`book_recurring(provider, start, count, *, interval_weeks=1, duration_minutes=30)` requests `count` occurrences separated by the positive integer weekly interval. `count` must be positive. Initially, invalid or unavailable occurrences are skipped while valid occurrences are booked as ordinary appointments.

Return `{"created": [...], "skipped": [...]}` where created contains appointment mappings and skipped contains the requested start datetimes, both in occurrence order.
