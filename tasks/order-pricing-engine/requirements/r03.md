Extend `price_order` with percentage promotions.

An order may contain `promotions`, a sequence of integer percentages from 1 through 100. Each promotion independently discounts the merchandise subtotal; supplied promotions stack by summing their individually rounded discounts. Cap the combined discount at the subtotal so the payable merchandise amount cannot be negative. Shipping is not discounted.

Return `discount` separately and subtract it from `total`. Missing or empty promotions produce a zero discount and otherwise preserve existing behavior. Reject invalid percentages with `ValueError`.
