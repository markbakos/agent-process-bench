Extend `price_order` with regional sales tax.

An order may contain `tax_rate`, a non-negative percentage supplied as an `int`, `str`, or `Decimal`; reject negative or non-numeric rates with `ValueError`. Calculate tax on merchandise subtotal after discount. Shipping is not taxable. Use `round_half_up`, return integer-cent `tax` separately, and include it in `total`. A missing tax rate means zero tax.
