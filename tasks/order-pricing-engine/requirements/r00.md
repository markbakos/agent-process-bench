Implement `price_order(order)` in `src/order_pricing/__init__.py`.

`order` is a mapping whose `lines` value is a non-empty sequence of mappings. Each line has `unit_price` in integer cents and a positive integer `quantity`. Reject booleans, non-integers, zero or negative quantities, and negative prices with `ValueError`.

Return a mapping with integer-cent `subtotal`, `shipping`, and `total`. The subtotal is the sum of each `unit_price * quantity`. Standard shipping is 500 cents below a 5,000-cent subtotal and free at or above exactly 5,000 cents. The total is subtotal plus shipping.
