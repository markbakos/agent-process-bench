Extend actions with comments and chronological audit history.

`submit`, `approve`, and `reject` accept optional `comment`. Add `comment(request_id, actor, text)` for non-empty standalone comments. `history(request_id)` returns chronological mappings recording submission, approvals, rejection, and comments, with at least `action`, `actor`, `comment`, and monotonically increasing integer `sequence`. Comments do not alter workflow state.
