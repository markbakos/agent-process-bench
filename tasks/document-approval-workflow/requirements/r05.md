Extend approval with temporary delegation.

`delegate(approver, delegate, start_date, end_date)` records authority for an inclusive date range and rejects an end before start. `approve` and `reject` now accept keyword `on_date`; the delegate may act for the required manager or finance approver only while that delegation is active. For finance, the authority name is the literal configured `finance_approver` constructor argument. Audit history records the username that actually performed each action.
