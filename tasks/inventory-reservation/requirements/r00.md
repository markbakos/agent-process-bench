Implement `Inventory(initial_stock)` in `src/inventory_reservation/__init__.py`.

`initial_stock` maps `(sku, warehouse)` tuples to non-negative integer quantities. `reserve(sku, warehouse, quantity)` accepts a positive integer quantity, rejects demand above current availability, and returns a reservation mapping with a stable unique string `id` and `status == "active"`. Active reservations reduce `availability(sku, warehouse)`. `release(id)` restores availability and `commit(id)` consumes the held physical stock. Unknown IDs raise `KeyError`; committing or releasing a non-active reservation raises `ValueError`. No operation may produce negative physical stock.
