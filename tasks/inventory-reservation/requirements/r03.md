Revise lifecycle visibility.

`commit` and `release` no longer make reservations disappear. They set status to `committed` or `released`, respectively. Add `get_reservation(id)` returning the reservation mapping for every lifecycle status. Only active reservations affect held demand and availability. Committing still consumes the reservation quantity from physical stock only up to the available on-hand amount; any uncovered portion remains a fulfilled backorder and physical stock floors at zero.
