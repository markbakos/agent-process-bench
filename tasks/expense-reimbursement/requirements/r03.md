Extend claims with deterministic multi-currency items.

`ExpenseEngine` accepts `base_currency` (default `"USD"`). An item may specify `currency`, defaulting to the base. `submit` accepts keyword `exchange_rates`, mapping currency codes to deterministic base-currency units per foreign unit as `int`, `str`, or `Decimal`. Convert each foreign amount to base cents with half-up rounding. Base-currency items remain unchanged. Receipt, cap, total, and approval calculations use converted base-currency cents. Never fetch live rates.
