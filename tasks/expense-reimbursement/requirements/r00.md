Implement `ExpenseEngine()` in `src/expense_reimbursement/__init__.py` using `datetime.date` and integer cents.

`submit(employee, items)` accepts a non-empty sequence of mappings containing `category`, `date`, `merchant`, `amount_cents`, and optional `receipt` boolean. Amounts must be positive integers. Any item over 2,500 cents requires a receipt. Every valid claim returns a stable unique mapping with `status == "pending_manager"` and `total_cents`.

Approved reimbursement in the item's calendar month is capped at 100,000 cents per employee. Reject a claim whose total plus already approved claims for any affected month exceeds the cap. `approve_manager(claim_id)` marks it approved and counts it against the appropriate item months. `get(claim_id)` returns the current claim mapping. Invalid claims or transitions raise without mutation.
