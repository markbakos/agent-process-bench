Revise foreign-currency rounding.

For each claim, group foreign item amounts by currency, convert each currency group's aggregate amount at high `Decimal` precision, and half-up round only that group total to base cents. For allocation, floor each item's exact converted amount, then distribute the remaining cents one at a time in input order until allocations sum to the rounded group total. Do not independently round each foreign item before summing. Base-currency items remain unchanged. Claim totals, duplicate normalization, thresholds, and approval use the allocated amounts.
