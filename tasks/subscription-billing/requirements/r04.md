Revise invoice credit handling.

Add `credit_customer(customer_id, cents)` for a positive integer credit. Invoice generation consumes available customer credit before money is due. Report the pre-credit charge as `subtotal`, the applied amount as `credits`, and floor `amount_due` at zero. Any credit beyond the charge remains available from `customer_credit(customer_id)` and is consumed by later invoices. Existing invoice idempotency must prevent consuming credit twice.
