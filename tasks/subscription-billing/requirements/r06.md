Extend invoice generation with jurisdictional tax.

`subscribe` accepts keyword `tax_rate`, a non-negative percentage supplied as an `int`, `str`, or `Decimal`. Apply tax after credits to the remaining taxable amount and round half-up to integer cents. Return `subtotal`, `credits`, `tax`, and `amount_due` separately, where amount due is post-credit amount plus tax. Missing tax rate means zero; reject invalid or negative rates.
