Implement `BillingEngine(plans)` in `src/subscription_billing/__init__.py` using `datetime.date` and integer cents. `plans` maps plan IDs to positive monthly prices.

`subscribe(customer_id, plan_id, signup_date)` returns a stable unique subscription ID. Monthly billing is anchored on the signup day; when absent in a month, clamp to that month's final day. `next_boundary(id, after)` returns the first boundary strictly after `after`. `generate_invoice(id, period_start)` requires a billing boundary and returns one stable mapping per subscription/period containing `id`, `subscription_id`, `period_start`, `subtotal`, `credits`, `tax`, and `amount_due`; repeated generation returns the original invoice.

`upgrade(id, new_plan_id, effective_date)` takes effect immediately and returns a prorated invoice for the remaining calendar days in the current billing period, including the effective date. Proration is the new-minus-old monthly price times remaining days divided by period days, rounded half-up to cents. Positive differences are due; negative differences are credits. The active plan changes immediately.
