Extend scheduling with fixed-interval recurring jobs.

`schedule` accepts keyword `recurrence_seconds`, a positive integer. The supplied ID is the series ID; occurrences have IDs `series_id#1`, `series_id#2`, and so on, each with independent attempts and ordinary job status. Schedule the next occurrence at the previous occurrence's original due time plus the interval after that occurrence succeeds or reaches terminal failure. Initially recurrence continues even after terminal failure. `get` accepts occurrence IDs.
