Implement `JobScheduler(base_delay_seconds=60, max_attempts=3)` in `src/retry_job_scheduler/__init__.py` using naive logical `datetime` values and no threads or sleeping.

`schedule(job_id, due_at)` creates a pending job and rejects duplicate IDs. `pop_due(now)` returns and marks running the next runnable job mapping, or `None`; order is earliest due time then FIFO insertion order. `record_result(job_id, success, finished_at)` marks success terminally. A failure retries with exponential delays `base * 2^(attempt_number-1)` after attempts 1 through `max_attempts - 1`; the attempt count increments when popped. Failure at the maximum makes status `failed`. Successful and terminal jobs never run again. Expose `get(job_id)`.
